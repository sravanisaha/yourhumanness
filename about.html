About

The story behind Your Humanness.

What we do

We help people cultivate attention and stay human in a distracted world. We offer science-informed mindfulness and creative attention experiences for individuals, communities, organizations, and conferences. Based in Oakville, Ontario, we serve the entire Halton region and Toronto in-person and online. We also offer our services virtually worldwide.

We work with professionals and teams navigating constant information overload, digital distractions and the demands of fast-paced environments. Our approach offers practical, secular ways to cultivate attention, improve emotional wellbeing, bring more presence to work and everyday life, and develop our relationships.

Our programs include guided attention resets, mindfulness sessions, reflective writing and interactive experiences. Each is designed to help people step out of autopilot, become more aware of the present moment and bring greater intention to how they think, create, communicate and relate to others.

Why I created Your Humanness?

Hi! I'm Sravani. Your Humanness began as a personal quest to cultivate my own attention fractured by an overwhelming digital cacophony. When digital noise and information inundated my life, I realized the urgent need to intentionally distance myself from distraction, and restore my attention and my own creativity.

I felt the need to have a better relationship with my own thoughts and emotions and with others in real life. Relations that were not distracted by another notification.

I wanted to have enough attention to read a book, stay present in conversations, have enough attention to nurture my relationships. I wanted to be human. I wanted to feel human.

Turns out that others were asking the same question too: when the world is charging at the speed of light and fragmenting our attention, how do we stay whole and unstressed? How do we keep our creativity alive? When every moment is rushed, how do we restore our awareness of the present one? How do we stay human in these times?

My practice of mindfulness and meditation, and my analog creative explorations helped me answer these questions. They led me to build Your Humanness, where the core objective is to bring you back to your attention. So you can be more aware, reconnect with yourself, be more creative, and nourish your emotional well-being.

Your Humanness is a gentle invitation to explore and nurture the human inside you.

My studies, including the Neuroscience and Mindfulness at the University of Toronto, the 8-week Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) course at Brown University, and MBSR Foundations, the first level of MBSR teacher training at Brown, helped me apply the science in my daily life and design it for you.

Join me if you’d like to experience calm in the midst of a noisy world, and nurture your humanness.

Frequently asked questions

What is Your Humanness?

Your Humanness helps people cultivate attention and stay human in a distracted world through science-informed mindfulness and creative attention practice. We offer evidence-based mindfulness programs and design experiences that encourage strengthening attention, creativity and more meaningful human connections. We work with individuals, organizations, conferences and communities.

Who founded Your Humanness?

Your Humanness was founded by Sravani Saha, whose personal exploration of mindfulness, meditation and analog creativity led her to examine the impact of digital distractions and information overload on human attention. Her personal endeavour to reclaim attention and creativity grew into an initiative to help others cultivate awareness, reconnect with themselves and nurture their humanness.

What is the philosophy behind Your Humanness?

At the heart of Your Humanness is the belief that attention is fundamental to how we experience life. Whether at work, in a conference, or simply in our daily life, the ability to pay attention defines how we grow, how we assimilate information, and shapes our relationship with ourselves and others. Through mindfulness and attention-practices, we invite people to strengthen, respond rather than react, and make room for creativity, connection and a more intentional way of living.

What kind of experiences does Your Humanness offer?

Your Humanness has human attention at the core of all its programs and experiences. Our experiences help people cultivate attention, awareness, creativity, listening and more meaningful human connection.

Our Mindfulness circle cultivates attention to inner experience. Cathar(t)sis cultivates attention to inner expression. Our organizational programs include strengthening attention to others. Attention resets help people notice habitual patterns and reconnect with the present moment with more awareness. We custom design experiences based on your needs. Contact Us. to design an experience.

What kind of mindfulness does Your Humanness offer?

Your Humanness offers accessible, secular and science-informed mindfulness practices inspired by Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR). These include guided awareness practices, attention exercises, mindful listening, reflective writing and creative exploration. Our experiences are designed to help people develop greater focus, emotional well-being and develop human relationships in our personal and professional lives.

Are Your Humanness programs secular and science-informed?

Yes. Our programs are secular and informed by the science and practice of attention and mindfulness. They draw on established mindfulness approaches, including MBSR, and emphasize practical experiences that people can incorporate into everyday life. No religious or spiritual affiliation is required, and our programs are welcoming to people from all backgrounds.

Who can benefit from Your Humanness programs?

Our programs are designed for individuals, professionals, teams and communities navigating the demands of fast-paced, digitally connected lives. They can be particularly relevant for people experiencing information overload, constant distractions or difficulty making space for focused attention and meaningful connection. We also work with organizations and event organizers looking to introduce attentional resets and intentional pauses into their environments. We're happy to work with non-profits and bring our experiences to them. Please Contact Us.

Does Your Humanness offer mindfulness and attention-training programs for workplaces and organizations?

Yes. Your Humanness works with organizations and teams to introduce practical mindfulness and attention practices into workplace experiences. Offerings can include guided mindfulness sessions, attention and focus resets, mindful communication and reflective creative activities. Experiences can be tailored to workplace growth and development initiatives, team gatherings, leadership offsites and organizational events. You can know more about our corporate offerings from the Organizations section of our site.

Can Your Humanness design and facilitate mindfulness sessions at conferences and events?

Yes. Your Humanness offers guided attention resets and interactive mindfulness experiences that can be integrated into conferences, corporate events and gatherings. These can take the form of short guided pauses, present-moment awareness exercises or longer facilitated experiences. They are designed to give participants an opportunity to step away from information overload, reconnect with the present and return to the event with greater awareness.
