About

The story behind Your Humanness.

What we do

We offer science-informed mindfulness and creative attention experiences for organizations, conferences and communities. Based in Oakville, Ontario, we serve the entire Halton region and Toronto in-person and online. We also offer our services virtually worldwide.

We work with professionals and teams navigating constant information overload, digital distractions and the demands of fast-paced environments. Our approach offers practical, secular ways to cultivate attention, develop greater awareness and bring more presence to work and everyday life.

Our programs include guided attention resets, mindfulness sessions, reflective writing and interactive experiences designed to help people pause, reconnect with the present moment and engage more intentionally.

Why we exist

Write to me directly at sravani@yourhumanness.com

Here’s what our founder, Sravani Saha, says about why she created Your Humanness.

Your Humanness began as a personal quest to cultivate my own attention fractured by an overwhelming digital cacophony. When digital noise and information inundated my life, I realized the urgent need to intentionally distance myself from distraction, and restore my attention and my own creativity.

I felt the need to have a better relationship with my own thoughts and emotions and with others in real life. Relations that were not distracted by another notification.

I wanted to have enough attention to read a book, stay present in conversations, have enough attention to nurture my relationships. I wanted to be human. I wanted to feel human.

Turns out that others were asking the same question too: when the world is charging at the speed of light and fragmenting our attention, how do we stay whole and unstressed? How do we keep our creativity alive? When every moment is rushed, how do we restore our awareness of the present one? How do we stay human in these times?

My practice of mindfulness and meditation, and my analog creative explorations helped me answer these questions. They led me to build Your Humanness, where the core objective is to bring you back to your attention. So you can be more aware, reconnect with yourself, be more creative, and nourish your emotional well-being.

Your Humanness is a gentle invitation to explore and nurture the human inside you.

My education in Neuroscience and Mindfulness from the University of Toronto, and in MBSR (Mindfulness Based Stress Reduction) along with Facilitator Credentials from Brown University empowered me to apply the science into my daily life and design it for you.

Join me if you’d like to experience calm in the midst of a noisy world, and nurture your humanness.

Our community

Community is at the heart of Your Humanness. Our Mindfulness Circle is a free, weekly online community gathering where people come together to practise mindfulness, cultivate awareness and experience the value of pausing together.

Whether you’re exploring mindfulness for the first time or looking to sustain an existing practice, you’re welcome to join.

Let’s connect

Whether you’re looking to build a practice for yourself, or bring an attention reset to a conference, introduce mindfulness to your workplace, organize a creative mindfulness experience or explore a community collaboration, we’d love to hear from you.

Let’s create an experience that helps people pause, reconnect and nurture their humanness.
