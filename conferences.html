For Conferences

Bring attention back into the room.

Conferences ask a great deal of attention: back-to-back sessions, constant input, and screens everywhere. Your Humanness weaves guided attentional resets into your event, so attendees stay present and return to every session with more focus.

Start a Conversation
Why it matters

Full schedules, depleted attention.

By mid-morning, most attendees are taking in more than they can hold. Attention is a trainable skill, and a few intentional minutes can change how people take part in the rest of the day.

Our work is designed for AI, marketing, tech and finance events, where the pace is fast and the cognitive load is high.

What we offer

Attentional resets, built into your programme.

Short guided resets

A few minutes of guided attention practice, placed at natural transition points: opening a session, after a keynote, or before a panel. Simple, secular, and led from the stage or the floor.

Extended guided sessions

A longer guided practice for a breakout, a morning start, or a quiet room open throughout the event. A space for attendees to settle and gather their attention.

Reflective writing

For events that want something more, a short creative or reflective writing exercise that helps attendees digest what they have heard and notice what stands out to them.

Sample integration

Woven into the day, not added on.

We design each reset around your schedule, audience and venue, so it feels like a natural part of the event rather than an interruption. Here is one way a full-day conference might use it.

We are happy to shape a different mix for your format, whether that is a half-day summit, a multi-day conference or a single stage.

An example day

Opening. A short guided reset to arrive and set the tone for the day.

Mid-morning. A brief reset between sessions to clear the first round of information.

After lunch. An extended guided session for the afternoon slump, or a quiet room open to all.

Closing. A short reflective writing exercise to help attendees take their key insights with them.

For organizers

Simple to include, easy to run.

We handle the facilitation and work with your team on timing and placement, so it adds very little to your production load.

Flexible format

Works on a main stage, in a breakout room, or on the expo floor. Short or long, in person or online, adapted to your event.

Minimal logistics

No special equipment is needed beyond a microphone and a quiet-enough space. We coordinate with your production team in advance.

Grounded in science

Our practices are adapted from MBSR and informed by science. Our experiences are secular and accessible to people with no prior mindfulness experience.
