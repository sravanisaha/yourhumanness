For Organizations

Attention is the foundation of good work.

Your Humanness brings science-informed attention training to teams and organizations, building the focus, composure and connection that sustainable performance depends on.

Work With Us
The challenge

Busy days leave little room to think.

Back-to-back meetings, constant messages and competing priorities pull attention in every direction. People move from task to task, often without the space to process, reflect or fully engage. Over time, fragmented attention shows up as reactivity, scattered focus, impacted decision-making and strained working relationships.

Attention is a skill, and it can be trained. Our sessions give teams practical ways to strengthen attention, drawn from science-informed mindfulness, to handle work stress.

Our approach

Three pillars at the center of our work.

Pillar one

Focus

Sustained, directed attention. Teams run sharper meetings, protect deep work, and make better decisions amid constant interruption.

Pillar two

Response

The ability to pause between trigger and reaction. Teams meet pressure, disagreement and change with clarity, reducing friction and reactivity.

Pillar three

Relationships

Listening with full attention. Teams build trust, communicate more clearly, and handle difficult conversations with care, the foundation of real collaboration.

What your organization gains

Attention, applied to how your team works.

Sharper focus and decision making

Teams remain focused, make high-stakes decisions without being stressed

Composure under pressure

People respond to deadlines, change and conflict deliberately rather than reactively.

Stronger collaboration

Better listening builds trust across teams, roles and levels of seniority.

Sustainable performance

A way to protect energy and attention that supports high performance without adding to burnout.

How it works

Simple to run, designed around you.

1. A conversation

We listen first, learning about your team, your pace and what you want to shift.

2. A tailored design

We shape the format, length and timing around your schedule, in person, virtual or hybrid, for small teams or larger groups.
